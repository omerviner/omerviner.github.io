My Cells and I
Description:
An interactive children's book that combines a mobile phone and takes the reader on a journey into the human body.
The book merges print and screen to create a unique educational experience, allowing children to explore body tissue, cells, organelles, and more through 3D pop-up elements and an interactive mobile app.
Results: raised ₪300K+ from about 2,200 backers on Headstart, shipped 2,000+ books, and won five international awards: Communication Arts Interactive Annual, Japan Media Arts Festival, Digital Ehon (two prizes) and iJungle Illustration. Content developed with the Davidson Institute of Science Education (Weizmann Institute).

My role: concept, illustration, Spine animation, the Unity app, print engineering and launch. The app recognizes the open page through the phone's camera, on-device, and plays its animated scene.

Technical information: Unity / C# (iOS, Android), Spine
