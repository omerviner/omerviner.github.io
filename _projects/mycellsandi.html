How it works

Inside the book

What I did

  • Concept and illustration. Every page, from body tissue to organelles to the fetus.
  • Animation. The animated characters inside each pop-up, made in Spine.
  • The app. Built in Unity for iOS and Android, with on-device recognition of the open page through the camera.
  • Print and launch. Paper engineering for the pop-ups, the Headstart campaign, and shipping the books.

Results

₪300K+ raised from about 2,200 backers on Headstart, and 2,000+ books shipped. Content was developed with the Davidson Institute of Science Education (Weizmann Institute).

Press

Made as a final project at the Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design, Jerusalem. Narrated by Amitay Gilad.
