What I did

  • Worked as part of a small team with a designer and another developer, plus product and backend. Took ownership of the features, the architecture and the technical decisions in my areas.
  • Built the coded design system: reusable components from buttons to full screens, with motion and layout primitives, documented in Storybook.
  • Designed the state architecture and data layer, moved it to Redux Toolkit, and tuned performance and memory for smooth editing on low-end devices.
  • Built canvas editing tools on Fabric.js, and shipped the UI in about 15 languages, including right-to-left.
