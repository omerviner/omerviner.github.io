The product

OrCam Hear is a pair of AI earbuds for people with mild hearing loss. It isolates the voices you choose and suppresses background noise in real time. Users control it from an iPhone app in the middle of a conversation, often at a busy table, so the app has to work at a glance.

My part

I was the developer of the iOS app and worked on the software on the earbuds' embedded device. The designers gave me the screens and the interaction model in Figma; I turned them into a working app connected to the device.

  • Figma to Swift. Implemented every screen pixel-accurate, and worked with the designers on motion, timing and edge cases the mockups didn't cover.
  • Components in code. Built each UI component in Swift as a shared part, so new screens were assembled from the same pieces as the design library.
  • Audio-driven animation. Animations react to live audio from the device, so users see who is being heard and that the earbuds are working.
  • Smooth interaction. Fast, predictable gestures for moving a person into or out of the conversation, tuned for use while talking.
  • State and data. Managed the app's state and linked the UI to the data layer that talks to the device: enrolled voices, who is active and device status.

What I built from

The design is a spatial model by Rony Dagan (UX) and Sarai Beris (UI): the room holds the people you're listening to, the balcony holds enrolled people who are waiting, and strangers stay outside. My job was to make that model feel immediate in the app.

Screens I built in Swift. Design: Rony Dagan and Sarai Beris.

On the device

  • Developed real-time C++ software for the embedded device: multi-process, multi-threaded, under tight compute and power limits.
  • Owned parts of the real-time audio-video flow: components that capture, process and deliver sound and image data.
  • Integrated ML algorithms; built performance optimizations, usage analytics and internal tools.
  • Coordinated development and testing with vision, DSP, algorithms, embedded, mobile and QA teams.
