The problem

[What wasn't working with the old site, and what the new one had to do.]

Scope

  • 31 designed screens: home, purchase, app welcome (from the QR code in the book), thank-you, press and 404, each for desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • A small design system and UI component sheet, a layered hero scene, share image, sign-up confirmation email and cookie consent.
  • Print pieces from the same system: a postcard for the package, a two-page teachers' guide and a "questions kids ask" sheet.
  • A purchase flow with quantities and add-ons, and a separate quote path for kindergartens and schools.

What's live

  • Home: a one-page story from the demo video and three spreads from the book, through the Davidson Institute endorsement, parents' and teachers' quotes, awards, press and an FAQ.
  • Purchase: one or two books, an optional bookmark and sticker add-on, pickup-point or home delivery, and secure checkout. Schools and kindergartens get a quote form for 5+ books.
  • Press, app welcome page (from the QR code in the book), legal pages and an accessibility statement.

Build

Astro on Cloudflare, Hebrew and right-to-left, responsive across phone, tablet and desktop. Forms with spam protection, a newsletter with double opt-in, cookie consent and ad measurement, a skip link and share previews on every page.
