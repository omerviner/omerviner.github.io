PopApp Books Website
Design and development, 2026
A new sales site for My Cells and I (אני והתא), designed in Claude Design and built by me. Hebrew, right-to-left, responsive across phone, tablet and desktop.
The problem
[What wasn't working with the old site, and what the new one had to do.]

Scope
• 31 designed screens: home, purchase, app welcome (from the QR code in the book), thank-you, press and 404, each for desktop, tablet and mobile.
• A small design system and UI component sheet, a layered hero scene, share image, sign-up confirmation email and cookie consent.
• Print pieces from the same system: a postcard for the package, a two-page teachers' guide and a "questions kids ask" sheet.
• A purchase flow with quantities and add-ons, and a separate quote path for kindergartens and schools.

Build
Astro and a Cloudflare Worker; responsive RTL layout, forms with spam protection, email deliverability, consent and analytics, accessibility and mobile performance.
